# Owner Legal Review Items — Alpha Public Policies

The public policy pages use restrained, implementation-grounded language and avoid unsupported business claims. The following decisions still need owner and qualified legal review before a broader commercial release:

1. **Legal entity and business name** — confirm the operating legal entity, any registered trade name, and whether a public business address must be published.
2. **Public legal contact method** — production currently exposes `absportscfb@gmail.com` as the support contact. Confirm it is the durable legal/privacy contact or provide a replacement.
3. **Minimum age and eligibility** — the signup flow does not enforce an age threshold or parent/guardian workflow. Decide and implement any required policy before asserting one publicly.
4. **Governing law and dispute resolution** — confirm any governing-law, venue, arbitration, class-action, or consumer-right language; none is asserted in the current Terms.
5. **Subscription and payment terms** — confirm terms, cancellation, and refund requirements before enabling paid subscriptions or purchases.
6. **Retention and deletion process** — establish documented retention periods, backup handling, and an account-deletion/data-request process beyond the present operational principles.
7. **Provider and data usage rights** — confirm the legal basis and applicable terms for each current and future sports-data source, particularly before public scale or commercial use.
8. **Privacy compliance scope** — review privacy-rights disclosures, cross-border processing, and any state, national, or international requirements that apply to the actual launch audience.
