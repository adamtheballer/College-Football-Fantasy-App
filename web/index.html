Common questions

College fantasy football FAQ

What is college fantasy football?

It is a season-long game where managers draft real college players, set lineups, and compete based on player production.

How does college fantasy football work?

Commissioners create a league, managers draft teams, then everyone sets a lineup and competes in weekly matchups.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a league, invite your managers, and run the draft and season from the same place.

Does CFFB have waivers and trades?

Yes. Managers can manage their rosters with waiver claims and league trades.
