Create a College Fantasy Football League

CFFB makes it simple to run a college fantasy football league with friends. Create your league, invite managers, draft real college players, and compete in weekly matchups all season.

Build a league for your group

A private league gives your group one place to organize the season. The commissioner creates the league, sets it up for the group, and sends invitations to managers. Every team then has its own roster, matchup schedule, league standing, and shared draft experience.

Draft together

The live draft room brings your managers into the same league event. Teams make picks from the available player pool, monitor the board, and build rosters that fit their strategy. After the draft, the roster becomes the starting point for every lineup choice and transaction.

Manage your roster through the season

College football rosters change every week. CFFB gives managers the tools to set a lineup, follow player performance, make waiver claims, and explore trades. Commissioners can manage league settings while each team focuses on the next matchup.

Compete in weekly fantasy matchups

Your league schedule creates a new head-to-head challenge each week. Follow scores and projected outcomes as games unfold, then see the impact in the standings once the week is complete. The goal is simple: build the best team and win your league’s season.
