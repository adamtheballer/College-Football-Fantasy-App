About CFFB

College Fantasy Football for real leagues

CFFB is a live college fantasy football platform built for people who want to run a complete league around the college football season.

What CFFB does

CFFB brings the core parts of a college fantasy football league together in one place. Managers can create or join a league, draft real college players, set a weekly lineup, follow head-to-head matchups, make waiver claims, discuss trades, and track the season with their friends.

Built around the college football season

College football moves on its own rhythm. CFFB is designed around the decisions that matter to a league: draft preparation, Saturday lineups, player availability, roster moves, weekly results, standings, and the ongoing competition between managers.

Live and ready for your league

CFFB is available now. Start a private league for your group, invite managers, and build the season together. The product is also available for iPhone on the App Store.

Bring your league to CFFB

Create a league to get started, or join one with an invitation from a commissioner.
